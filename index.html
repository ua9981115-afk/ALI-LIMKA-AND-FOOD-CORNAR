About Our Story

From a small dream to a thriving street food business...

Syed Limka and Food Cart started as a passion project to bring fresh, affordable, and delicious food to the vibrant community of Burns Road. What began as a humble food cart has grown into a beloved neighborhood staple, serving thousands of satisfied customers.

We believe that great food doesn't have to be expensive or complicated. Our commitment to quality ingredients, hygienic preparation, and exceptional customer service sets us apart. Every drink we serve is freshly prepared, and every meal is cooked with care.

We serve not just customers, but a community. From busy professionals grabbing a quick lunch to families enjoying weekend treats, we're honored to be part of your daily moments. Our mission is simple: serve the best, stay fresh, and keep smiling.
